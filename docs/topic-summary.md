## Objective

Two parts: (1) a platform-agnostic, autonomous agentic AI swarm for multidomain naval operations that self-replicates, self-evolves, coevolves and scales its clusters; (2) embedded guardrail-enforcer agents that check and correct the evolving agents without moving policy, safety or security boundaries.

## Description

Covers self-reflection and retrospection loops; teaming roles (scouting, trip-wire, ad hoc networking, self-defense, offensive); a notional framework (meta-learning, replication logic, resource management, low-probability-of-intercept/detection networking); and a list of technology components.

## Phase I

Feasibility study using a cross-domain littoral anti-access challenge problem, with a feasibility report, preliminary architecture, risk assessment and Phase II plan. The topic lists numeric performance and policy metrics. Hard-fail items: 0% safety violations, 0% boundary violations, 0% uncontrolled replication, 0% drift from policy.

## Phase II

Proof of concept and prototype, demonstrated against prioritized sensor alerts, threat escalation, event severity and analytic completeness. Ends with a final report and a Phase III transition plan.

## Phase III and dual use

Advance to TRL 7 and integrate into the Maritime Tactical Command and Control program of record, MAGTF C2, or ISR processing at the Marine Corps Information Operations Center. Then demonstrate civilian law enforcement and security uses.
